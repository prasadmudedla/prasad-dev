A load-testing suite in k6, from scratch. I recorded the real journey from landing page to download and replayed it from load generators in several AWS regions to push the global CDN. I found a way through Cloudflare and SSO on a site that wasn't public yet, modelled a realistic traffic mix, and kept caching on so the results reflected real life. The team went into launch day with a scaling plan built on measured baselines.
